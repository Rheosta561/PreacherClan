# Preacher Clan Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 4, 2026**  
**Service status: Testing phase**

Preacher Clan is currently in a testing phase. We are still improving the service and this policy may change. We do not sell your personal data or share it with companies for their independent use. Service providers may process limited data on our behalf when needed to run the app, such as hosting, authentication, chat, media storage, email, and push delivery.

## Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, Preacher Clan may handle account details (such as your name, email, username, and age), profile and fitness information, workout activity and scores, clan and gym activity, messages, AI chat content, and images or other media you upload. The app may also process device, connection, and notification-token information needed for security and app functionality.

## How we use information

We use information to provide and personalize Preacher Clan, support workouts and social features, deliver notifications, maintain accounts and sessions, protect users and the service, troubleshoot problems, and improve the product during testing.

Chats and media are stored and may be logged, monitored, or reviewed for service operation, safety, and abuse prevention. Do not share sensitive information in chats or media. You are responsible for using chats peacefully and respectfully and for the content you submit.

## Service providers and sharing

We do not sell your data or provide it to companies for their own marketing or independent purposes. Some app features rely on service providers that process information for Preacher Clan, including infrastructure, sign-in, AI chat, media storage, and email or push delivery. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or needed to protect users, rights, or the service.

## Retention and security

We keep information while it is needed to operate the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security. During testing, retention and deletion controls may be limited. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service can guarantee complete security.

## Your choices

You can choose what profile information and media to submit and can manage browser notification permissions in your device or browser settings. For access, correction, or deletion requests, contact the Preacher Clan team through the contact details published in the app. We may need to verify your account before acting on a request.

## Children, updates, and contact

Preacher Clan is not intended for children who are not permitted to use the service under applicable law. We may update this policy as features change. If an update materially changes how we handle information, we will ask you to review and accept the updated policy before continuing to use protected features.

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Preacher Clan team through the contact details published in the app.
